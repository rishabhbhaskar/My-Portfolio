## 🚀 SAP Business One (SAP B1) & Enterprise Highlights

1. **SAP B1 Full Implementation & Functional Support**:
   - Financials, Sales (AR), Purchasing (AP), Inventory & Stock Valuation, Production & MRP.
   - Formatted Searches (FMS), User-Defined Fields (UDF), User-Defined Tables (UDT), User-Defined Objects (UDO).
   - Transaction Notification stored procedures in HANA SQL for business rule enforcement.

2. **SAP B1 Service Layer & B1iF E-Commerce Integration**:
   - Bi-directional REST API integration using SAP B1 Service Layer & B1i Framework (B1iF).
   - Automated posting of AR Invoices, Incoming Payments, Item Master & Stock Sync.

3. **Eagle Enterprise (SAP SD/MM Support)**:
   - Post-go-live L2/L3 functional support, custom pricing routines, and sales order tracking reports (`ZSD_SO_TRACKING`).

4. **Spartan Industries (Order-to-Cash & STO)**:
   - Order-to-Cash (O2C) support, Automatic Credit Management with custom BADI checks (`BADI_SD_CM`), and STO customization.

5. **SD Bio & Esskay Beauty Care**:
   - Batch Management & Expiry alert reports (`ZSDBIO_BATCH_EXPIRY`), serial tracking, e-Way bill programs (`ZESSKAY_EWAY`), and promotional pricing.

6. **Crystal Reports & Custom Z-Reports**:
   - 60+ custom Crystal Reports and ABAP ALV reports across B1 and ECC/S4 platforms.
